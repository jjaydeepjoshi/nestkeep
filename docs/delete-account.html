Delete your NestKeep account and data

You can ask us to delete your NestKeep account and everything stored with it: buildings, tenants, bills, payments, and the photos and documents saved in your Google Drive app folder.

How to request deletion

  1. Email spikkle.learn@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "NestKeep account deletion".
  2. We delete your records and account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Delete files yourself right away

Open Google Account, Third-party apps and services, choose NestKeep, and select "Delete all connections". This removes the app's hidden Drive folder, including tenant photos and documents.
