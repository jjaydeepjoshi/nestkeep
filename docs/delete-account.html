Delete your NestKeep account and data

You can ask us to delete your NestKeep account and everything stored with it: buildings, tenants, bills, payments, and the photos and documents stored with it.

How to request deletion

  1. Email spikkle.learn@gmail.com from the Google account you used to sign in, with the subject "NestKeep account deletion".
  2. We delete your records and account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Delete files yourself right away

Open Google Account, Third-party apps and services, choose NestKeep, and select "Delete all connections". This revokes the app's access to your Google account. To have your stored data erased, email us from the address you signed in with.
